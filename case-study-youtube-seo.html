YouTube Video SEO & Algorithmic Ranking Optimization

Complete Before vs After YouTube video SEO overhaul — transforming a dormant 46-view release into an algorithm-recommended asset with 12 top-ranked keyword tags and 1,220+ targeted organic views.

YouTube SEO & Metadata Architecture
01 — The Challenge & Baseline Metrics

Stagnant organic distribution with 0 ranked keywords

The official music video "VOLAS - Rather Get Married" was uploaded without structured metadata. Within the first 17 hours, the video flatlined at just 46 views with an SEO score of 0/20 and zero ranked tags, leaving it invisible to YouTube's search and recommendation engines.

02 — SEO Architecture & Execution

Keyword research, high-volume tags & description schema

  • Competitor Keyword Gap Analysis: Identified high-intent search phrases and niche artist search queries via VidIQ & TubeBuddy.
  • Optimized Title & Description Copy: Structured keyword-dense description copy with verified timestamp chapters and artist links.
  • Strategic Tag Placement: Injected 12 top-performing search tags that triggered YouTube search ranking.
  • End Screen & Card Architecture: Configured interactive cards and end screens to retain viewers across the entire channel.
03 — Verified Results & Analytics

Analytical proof: 12 ranked tags and 1,220+ views
