01 — Product Overview

Accelerating legal contract workflows with precision AI

Legal teams spend hundreds of hours reviewing repetitive contract clauses. Robin AI pairs generative AI with specialized legal verification. The interface needed to balance futuristic AI capabilities with strict corporate privacy and ISO trust requirements.

02 — Key Architecture

3-Tier product offering & trust guarantees

  • AI Assistant Hero: Interactive contract chat drawer preview with robot visual framing.
  • 3 Product Tiers: Robin AI Review (contract redlining), Robin AI Query (instant legal search), and Robin AI Services (expert-in-the-loop validation).
  • Privacy & Security Guarantee: Prominent trust badges ensuring client contracts are never used to train public LLM models.
  • Global Client Map & Resource Hub: Case studies, webinars, and partner ecosystem.
